Deployment lanes

Target-state architecture: the same protocol boundaries apply across operator-hosted, business-managed and enterprise deployments. Prefer decentralised systems when they meet the operation's confidentiality, consistency, recovery and service requirements.

Community sovereign node
The operator controls hosting, upgrades and operational identity.
Business-managed service
The managed operator provides hosting and support under explicit commercial terms. Hosting does not confer unilateral authority over participant funds.
Enterprise or private cloud
The customer controls infrastructure and identity roots within its own perimeter.

Shared boundaries

  • Protocol truth remains anchored to on-chain policy and verified proofs. Off-chain projections remain derived.
  • Dashboards observe and propose; production signing remains a separate, qualified authority.
  • Changes follow repository review and release acceptance. A deployed container does not prove an operation is qualified.
  • Public documentation can be independently mirrored. Private knowledge bases, customer records and confidential deal documents are excluded.

Research and interoperable contributions are welcome through GitHub issues and pull requests. New providers require operation-specific evidence and an accountable maintainer.
